3. Hardware connections
The MFRC522 communicates using SPI. Connect it as follows:
MFRC522 pin	Raspberry Pi connection
SDA / SS	GPIO8, physical pin 24
SCK	GPIO11, physical pin 23
MOSI	GPIO10, physical pin 19
MISO	GPIO9, physical pin 21
IRQ	Not connected
GND	Physical pin 6 (GND)
RST	GPIO25, physical pin 22
3.3V	Physical pin 1 (3.3V)

from mfrc522 import SimpleMFRC522

reader = SimpleMFRC522()

try:
    print("Place your RFID card on the reader")
    print("Press Ctrl+C to stop")

    while True:
        print("\nChoose an option:")
        print("1. Read RFID card")
        print("2. Write data to RFID card")

        choice = input("Enter choice (1 or 2): ")

        if choice == "1":
            print("Reading card...")
            card_id, text = reader.read()
            print("Card ID:", card_id)
            print("Stored data:", text)

        elif choice == "2":
            data = input("Enter text to write: ")
            print("Place your card on the reader...")
            reader.write(data)
            print("Data written successfully!")

        else:
            print("Invalid choice")

except KeyboardInterrupt:
    print("\nProgram stopped")

finally:
    print("RFID program ended")
